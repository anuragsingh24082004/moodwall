Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to MoodWall. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be processed through your use of our application. This policy explains how we handle your data to ensure a safe and trustworthy experience.

2. Information Collection

MoodWall is designed to be privacy-first. We only request access to features necessary for the app's functionality:

  • Camera Access: Used exclusively for real-time facial expression and color detection.
  • Sensor Access: Used for gesture-based features like "Shake to Change."
  • Purchase Data: We use Google Play Billing to verify and restore your premium feature ownership.

We do not collect or store unnecessary personal identification data.

3. Camera Usage

Our app uses the device camera to enable the interactive Panda and dynamic wallpaper features. We value your trust in this regard:

  • No Storage: Facial data, photos, and videos are processed in real-time and are never stored on your device or uploaded to any server.
  • Local Processing: All facial analysis and color detection happen 100% locally on your device. No data ever leaves your phone.
  • Real-Time Only: The camera is active only when the wallpaper is visible or during preview setup.

4. Data Storage

MoodWall does not store personal media (photos/videos). Any data stored locally is limited to your app settings, preferences, and cached assets required for the wallpaper's performance.

5. Third-Party Services

To provide a high-quality experience, we may use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • AdMob (for reward-based ad features)
  • Firebase Analytics (for improving app stability)

6. Children’s Privacy

MoodWall does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our interactive features are designed for general audiences and do not store or transmit any identifiable data.

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your local preferences and ensure the integrity of the application. Since we do not collect personal data on our own servers, the risk of a data breach is minimized.

8. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage users to check this page periodically for any changes. Your continued use of the app signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.
